How does a streak work?

A day counts when your focused minutes for that day reach 15. The streak is how many counting days you have in a row. Today does not fail the streak before the day is over. Minutes from a session count on the day the session started.

A day still counts toward the streak at 15 focused minutes, even if your daily goal is higher.

You start with 2 streak freezes and can hold 3. A missed day spends one freeze and the streak stays. With none left, the streak resets. Each time the streak lands on a multiple of 7, you earn one freeze back if you are under the cap. Today does not spend a freeze before the day is over.

Allowing Screen Time

If you allow Screen Time, Apple asks you to confirm. You can then pick the apps or categories to shield during a focus session.

You can skip this and still use Nix as a timer. The timer, XP, and streak still work. Shields stay off until you allow Screen Time and pick apps.

How do I add the widget?

On the Home Screen, touch and hold the wallpaper, tap the plus button, and search for Nix: Focus Streaks.

The widget shows your current streak and how many minutes you have focused today. It updates when a session finishes. The widget reads the same on-device snapshot as the app.

How do I reset progress?

Open About & Help in the app and choose Reset progress on this phone. Sessions, XP, and the streak are deleted on this device. Screen Time access and the apps you picked stay as they are.

You can also delete the app to remove its data from this device.

Privacy

Nix stores your sessions, streak, and settings on your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Nix never sees which apps you open. Apple keeps that private.
