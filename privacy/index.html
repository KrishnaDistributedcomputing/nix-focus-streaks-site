Nix: Focus Streaks Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Data and processing

Nix: Focus Streaks processes your focus sessions, streak, XP, and settings so you can time focus sessions and keep a streak on your device.

This data is stored on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you reset progress, which deletes sessions, XP, and the streak on that device.

The app does not send this data to a server and does not sync it across devices.

Accounts, analytics, advertising and tracking: There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Third parties and their purposes: The app does not share your data with third-party companies. It uses Apple system features on the device, as described below, only when you choose to use them.

Optional permissions

Screen Time

If you allow Screen Time, Apple asks you to confirm. You pick the apps or categories to shield. Nix saves the opaque tokens Apple provides and, during a focus session, asks the system to shield those apps. Nix cannot see your other usage, the content of any app, or a history of what you opened.

You can say no. The timer, XP, and streak still work. Shields stay off until you allow Screen Time and pick apps.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, this device schedules a local notification. That stays on the device.

Widgets

The Home Screen widget reads an on-device snapshot of your streak and today's minutes. It uses a shared container on the same device. The widget cannot access data beyond that snapshot.

Purchases, exports and support

Purchases: Nix: Focus Streaks has no in-app purchases and does not process payments.

Exports: The app does not offer a data export. Your data stays on the device until you delete it as described below.

Support: If you email for support, you choose what to include in your message. That email is used only to respond to your request.

Choices and deletion

You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings. You can revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings. Shields then stay off.

Delete the app to remove its data from this device. You can also reset progress from About & Help. That clears sessions, XP, and the streak on this device. It does not remove Screen Time access or the apps you picked.

If you back up this device with Apple, a copy of the app's on-device data may be included in that backup until you delete the backup or the app's data. The app itself does not upload data to iCloud or any other service.

Nix: Focus Streaks is not a medical or fitness app. It does not collect children's data.
